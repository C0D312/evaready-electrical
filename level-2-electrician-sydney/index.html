When To Call

When do you need a Level 2 electrician?

If the electrical work involves the service connection, consumer mains, defect notices or supply-side equipment, you may need Level 2 electrical support.

You received an electrical defect notice

Your consumer mains are old, damaged or undersized

Your switchboard needs to be upgraded for extra load

You are renovating, rebuilding or upgrading your property

Your overhead or underground supply needs work

You need metering or service-related electrical work

Level 2 authority

Supply-side work needs more than general electrical knowledge.

Network requirements

If a job involves Ausgrid or Endeavour Energy supply requirements, it should be scoped through the correct Level 2 process for that network area.

Defect notices

A defect notice may involve consumer mains, point of attachment, private poles, service fuses, metering or switchboard issues that need prompt attention.

Supply upgrades

Renovations, EV chargers, larger appliances and three-phase enquiries can all trigger a need to review the property supply and switchboard capacity.

Overhead and underground services

Service lines, point of attachment issues and underground supply problems need careful inspection, planning and safe isolation where required.

Paperwork and compliance

Some supply-side work may require CCEW documentation, defect evidence, photos, testing details and a clear record of what was rectified.

Temporary supply and protection

Builders supply, tiger tail coordination, service fuse enquiries and private pole issues should be planned early so the site can be kept moving safely.

Level 2 FAQ

Common Level 2 questions.

What is Level 2 electrical work?

Level 2 electrical work generally involves service equipment, consumer mains, metering, overhead or underground supply work, defect notices and connection-related electrical tasks.

What should I send with a defect notice enquiry?

Send a clear photo of the defect notice, the switchboard, any point of attachment or private pole, your suburb and the deadline listed on the notice.

Can Level 2 work be needed for EV chargers?

Sometimes. EV chargers can require switchboard, load or supply upgrades, so the existing service and consumer mains may need to be assessed.
