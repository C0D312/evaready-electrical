Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Evaready Electrical handles customer contact details, booking information, photos, documents and website data when you call, request a quote or use this website.

Last updated: 2 June 2026

Who we are

Evaready Electrical provides electrical services across Sydney and surrounding regions. Our website helps customers contact us, request quotes, send job details and understand the services that may suit their electrical issue.

What information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, business name, suburb, job notes, preferred contact details and any other information you choose to provide when you call, email or submit an enquiry.

If you send photos, documents, defect notices, switchboard photos, meter box photos or other job information, we collect that material so we can understand the request and discuss the next step with you.

How we collect information

Information may be collected when you use the secure booking form, call us, email us, click a website link, send photos or documents, or communicate with us about an electrical job.

We may also collect basic website information such as pages visited, device type, browser type, referring website and general interaction data through analytics, advertising tags and hosting tools.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, manage booking requests, assess job details, contact customers, arrange work, keep basic job records and improve the website experience.

For urgent faults such as no power and burning smells, sparking, wet electrical equipment or repeated tripping, you should call directly rather than relying on a form submission.

Photos, documents and job details submitted through the quote form

Photos and documents can help us understand the electrical issue, site access, switchboard condition, defect notice details or the type of planned work requested. Only send information that is relevant to the job.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal documents unless they are needed for the job. If you send information about another person or property, make sure you have authority to provide it.

Booking and service providers

We may use trusted third-party tools to manage enquiries, bookings, customer communication, website hosting, email, analytics and business administration. This can include ServiceM8 or similar booking and administration tools where relevant.

These providers may process information only for the purpose of helping us operate the website, manage customer enquiries and provide electrical services.

Website analytics and advertising tags

This website may use analytics and advertising technologies, including Google Ads and the Google tag, to understand website performance, measure advertising activity and improve future customer enquiries.

These tools may use cookies or similar technologies. The information collected is generally used in aggregated or advertising measurement form, not to sell your personal information.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to keep the website working, measure traffic, understand how visitors use pages and support advertising measurement. You can manage cookies through your browser preferences.

When information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with booking, administration, hosting, email, analytics or trade service providers where needed to respond to your enquiry or manage the requested work.

Information may also be shared if required by law, safety, compliance, payment, dispute handling or authority-related electrical work.

How information is stored

We take reasonable steps to keep customer information secure using the systems and providers that support our booking, email, website and administration processes. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access, correction and deletion requests

You can ask to access, correct or request deletion of personal information we hold about you. Some information may need to be kept for business, safety, legal, compliance or record-keeping reasons.

Contact details

For privacy questions, access requests or corrections, contact Evaready Electrical by email at info@evareadyelectrical.com.au or call 0461 247 247.
