## Decision rule

The repository currently contains no provenance-backed completed-job record, local job photograph or verified testimonial for any of the 873 suburb pages. The queue below identifies sensible pages to review first; it does not claim search volume, lead volume, revenue, ranking strength or work previously completed in those suburbs.

Before final ordering, the owner should compare each candidate against:

- Completed-job history and available photo evidence.
- Google Search Console impressions, clicks and query intent.
- Google Ads search terms and qualified lead outcomes.
- ServiceM8 enquiries, completed jobs and service value.
- Existing backlinks, referrals and repeat-customer demand.
- Permission to publish the proposed job facts and images.

A page moves ahead only when genuine evidence exists. Do not manufacture an example merely because a suburb appears in this queue.

1. Sydney and Eastern Suburbs.
2. Parramatta and Cumberland.
3. Canterbury-Bankstown and Inner South West.
4. Northern Sydney and Ryde.
5. Western Sydney and Nepean.
6. Liverpool and Fairfield.
7. Hills, Hawkesbury and Hornsby.
8. One approved selected outer-region example, if genuine evidence and service limitations are clear.

This is an editorial coverage sequence only. Real completed work and owner approval always take priority.

## Indexation recommendation for owner review

- Keep all existing routes unchanged during evidence collection.
- Do not mass-noindex, delete, redirect or canonicalise pages from similarity alone.
- Strengthen priority pages with verified evidence only when a real record exists.
- Monitor impressions, clicks, qualified leads and crawl/index status after launch.
- Review persistently low-value pages individually using search performance, backlinks, customer usefulness and the availability of a truthful replacement route.
- Make any future noindex, consolidation or redirect decision in a separate owner-approved task with a route mapping and rollback plan.

## Definition of done for one priority page

A priority page is evidence-ready only when its job facts, month, completed services, photograph rights, alt text, review source (if used) and explicit public-use approval are complete in `docs/location-evidence-owner-input-template.md`. A technical route with no approved record remains honest but is not considered locally evidenced.
