# Location Evidence Review Inputs

Status: OWNER DATA REQUIRED - NO ROUTE PRIORITY HAS BEEN ASSIGNED

## What the repository can establish

The repository confirms 873 valid suburb routes, their suburb/postcode hierarchy, current response classification, service pathways, nearby links and conversion actions. It currently contains no approved completed-job evidence, local job photograph or verified testimonial for any suburb page.

Geographic size, familiarity or position in the route hierarchy does not prove search demand, qualified leads, revenue, completed work or commercial value. Earlier geographic review queues must not be treated as a priority ranking.

## Owner exports required for genuine prioritisation

Priority decisions require owner-controlled evidence that is not available in this repository:

- Google Search Console impressions, clicks and relevant queries by route.
- Google Ads qualified conversion data and matching search intent.
- ServiceM8 enquiries and completed jobs, aggregated without customer details.
- Revenue or margin priority supplied by the owner.
- Google Business Profile evidence relevant to the service area.
- Legitimate backlinks, referrals and partner citations by route.
- Confirmed serviceability and response capability.
- Approved completed-job, photograph or testimonial evidence.

Do not commit raw exports containing customers, employees, addresses, phone numbers, emails, job/invoice references, account details or access information. The owner should aggregate and sanitise route-level totals before handoff.
