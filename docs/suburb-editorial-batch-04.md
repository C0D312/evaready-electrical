## Meaning and Originality

All 55 researched entries were screened against all 873 suburb outputs and
their original pre-editorial templates using the unchanged seven-word coverage
method and 30% threshold. Place names/postcodes are normalised. Shared safety
and FAQ text remains in the measurement. This is an internal screen, not a
Google rule or proof that every URL merits indexing.

The new twenty pages score 37.236% to 39.366% distinct against their closest
full-corpus text matches and 39.245% to 41.045% against the old templates. Across
all 55 assessed pages, the corresponding ranges are 36.299% to 39.366% and
37.958% to 41.045%. The previous 35 still pass; no historic content was rewritten
to manipulate this result.

The new bodies and their closest relationships were assessed for usefulness,
not merely different vocabulary. The previous 35 full reviews were retained
and their expanded closest-neighbour relationships checked. Strong overlaps
include Ryde/Ashfield on inspection limits, Blacktown/Prestons on combined loads
and Seven Hills/Smithfield on installation versus machine work. Those are
legitimate shared customer questions in independently verified contexts, not
exclusive local fault claims. No arbitrary replacement topic or invented local
job was added to manufacture novelty. Consolidation, demand and indexation
decisions remain held pending appropriate evidence.
