## Scope and sources

Twenty pending routes were selected before writing for geographic coverage and
available primary-source context, not commercial ranking or confirmed
serviceability. No substitutions. Only `data/suburb-editorial.ts` changes
production content. The renderer, 768px reading column, header, backgrounds,
artwork, styles, controllers, navigation, routes and indexation policy are held.

Each page links its own ABS 2021 all-persons Suburbs and Localities table. Housing
percentages use occupied private dwellings, excluding visitor-only and other
non-classifiable households. They are historical context, not current electrical
condition, demand, property ownership, tenure, jobs or safety evidence. Conditional
advice describes the customer's actual situation; no suburb is assigned a
characteristic fault or an unverified specialist service.

## Manual closest-match review

The unchanged seven-word coverage screen includes all 873 outputs and compares
each researched page with its original pre-editorial template. The 30% threshold
is an internal editorial screen, not a Google requirement or ranking guarantee.
Manual review also checks meaning, not just statistics or synonyms.

- Belfield/Revesby: building-work dependencies versus lighting/dimmer compatibility.
- Belmore/Lakemba: observing a recurring fault versus permission for a planned alteration.

- Earlwood/Campsie: existing house records and staged building work versus apartment access.

- Wetherill Park/Moorebank: future expansion dependencies versus existing damage restrictions.
- Casula/Belfield: separate dwelling/supply boundaries versus whole-project sequencing.

- Prestons/Revesby: combined new loads and future assumptions versus a lighting/control combination.

- Dulwich Hill/Casula: coordinating water-source repairs and electrical clearance versus dwelling identification and access.
