These notes record the primary public sources used for the individual review of
the six Phase 3D3 routes. Sources were reviewed on 5 September 2026. They guide
safety and responsibility wording; they do not prove owner credentials,
response times, completed jobs or other business claims.

## Route decisions

### Emergency electrician

The route now places immediate safety action before its first page CTA, fixes
an ambiguous call-flow sentence and separates emergency services, public
network assets and property-side electrical work. Existing owner-approved
availability and Level 2 wording is retained without expansion.

### Electric shock

The route separates medical care from electrical investigation. It tells users
not to touch a person who may remain connected, identifies when to call Triple
Zero (000), recommends medical assessment after any shock and limits Evaready's
role to the electrical hazard. It does not provide diagnosis from a symptom.

### RCD and safety-switch repairs

The route now focuses on active or repeated tripping, testing and repair. It is
kept distinct from the existing safety-switch installation page, which covers
planned new protection. The repair page does not assume that tripping proves a
protective device has failed.

### Storm damage

The existing service guide already covered moisture, private service
equipment, network assets, make-safe work, other-trade boundaries and planned
follow-up. The introduction was still changed because immediate safety and 000
guidance needed to precede the first page CTA.

### Switchboard upgrades

The route now distinguishes fault repair, circuit-protection work and a broader
upgrade. It explains that replacing a switchboard does not by itself increase
the available supply and identifies network, metering, asbestos, access and
making-good boundaries without promising a standard upgrade package.

### Three phase power

The route now starts with equipment and demand rather than assuming three phase
is required. It distinguishes property circuits, switchboard work, metering,
retailer and distributor responsibilities and any separately authorised
supply-side work.

## Claim and safety limits

- No new price, discount, warranty, insurance, response-time, review-rating or
  completed-job claim was added.
- No route tells a customer to open a switchboard, bypass protection or perform
  electrical work.
- The existing public response-time and specialist credential wording remains
  governed by the owner-verification register.
- Network, medical, appliance, building and other non-electrical
  responsibilities are described only to make the service boundary clear.
