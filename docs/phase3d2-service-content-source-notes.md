# Phase 3D2 service-content source notes

The six Phase 3D2 rewrites use plain-language summaries of current official
Australian and NSW guidance. They do not reproduce paid standards or turn a
general web page into a site-specific compliance determination.

## Sources reviewed

- NSW Government, [Electrical safety in the home](https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/safety-home/electrical-safety/electrical-safety-home): licensed electrical work, circuit-breaker and safety-switch roles, and general household electrical safety.
- NSW Government, [Safety switches protect you](https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/safety-home/electrical-safety/safety-switches-protect-you): safety-switch purpose and the need for licensed electrical work.
- Healthdirect, [Electric shocks and burns](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/electric-shocks-and-burns): immediate-danger and Triple Zero guidance after electric shock.
- Ausgrid, [EV charging](https://www.ausgrid.com.au/transforming-the-grid/innovating-for-the-future/electric-vehicles/ev-charging): licensed-contractor assessment, premises wiring and possible connection-application requirements.
- NSW Climate and Energy Action, [Install a home electric vehicle charger](https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/households/transport/electric-vehicles/home-charger): charger types and licensed-electrician installation guidance.
- NSW Climate and Energy Action, [Making your residential strata building EV ready](https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/business-and-industry/programs-grants-and-schemes/electric-vehicles/electric-vehicle-ready/strata): building capacity, common-property approval and load-management considerations.
- SafeWork NSW, [Lighting](https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/hazards-a-z/lighting): emergency-lighting purpose, visibility, inspection, testing and maintenance.
- National Construction Code, [Part E4: Visibility in an emergency, exit signs and warning systems](https://ncc.abcb.gov.au/editions/ncc-2022/adopted/volume-one/e-services-and-equipment/part-e4-visibility-emergency-exit-signs-and-warning-systems): evacuation visibility and exit-identification objectives and the fact that requirements depend on the building.
- NSW Government, [Heat pump installations for electrical work](https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/compliance-and-regulation/electricians/electrical-standards-rules-and-notes/heat-pump-installations-for-electrical-work): electrical protection, isolation and moisture considerations for heat-pump hot-water systems.
- NSW Energy Sustainability Schemes, [Heat-pump water-heater legislative licence requirements](https://www.energysustainabilityschemes.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/ess_documents/Notice-03-2024-Requirements-Heat-Pump-Water-Heater-Scheme-Legislative-Licence-Requirements.PDF): separate electrical, plumbing and refrigerant licence scopes.

## Content boundaries

The public wording:

- uses “our licensed electricians” for ordinary electrical work;
- treats symptoms as possible causes rather than certain diagnoses;
- places immediate safety action before the first page-level conversion action;
- identifies network, strata, fire-safety, plumbing and refrigerant boundaries without claiming credentials that are not required for the selected service;
- avoids response-time, insurance, price, discount, warranty, review and outcome claims;
- avoids dangerous do-it-yourself instructions; and
- preserves the existing Call, Quote, ServiceM8, metadata, schema and route implementation.
