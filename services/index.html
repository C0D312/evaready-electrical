Trust and process

How Evaready scopes electrical services.

For electrical services across Sydney and surrounding regions, Evaready uses photos, job notes and call-first safety triage to confirm the clearest next action.

For burning smells, smoke, sparking, power loss, wet electrical equipment or unsafe wiring, call first. For life-threatening danger, keep clear and call emergency services first.

Licence verification

NSW Electrical Licence 398937C, ABN 44 650 697 797, Open Cabler registration number 46691 and ARCtick L157323 are shown clearly before you call or book.

Emergency call triage

Clear next actions

Urgent faults start with the phone. Planned work is reviewed from the details you send before the next action is confirmed.
